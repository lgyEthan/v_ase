# Desktop interface references

Reviewed on 9 November 2026 with a dedicated GPT-6 Astra (xhigh) design reviewer. Twelve applications were examined; ten have specific editorial, award, customer or community praise linked below. This is a source catalog, not a popularity ranking. Historical reviews establish design lineage; individual reviews and community comments do not establish consensus.

Pixelmator Pro and Photos define the workspace composition. Arc and Nova inform spatial continuity, Raycast command access, Craft control feedback, and CleanShot the export-result lifecycle. All shipped graphics, styles and motion are original. Reference screenshots and video samples are review evidence only.

## Evidence boundaries

**Apple Photos.** Official Photos screenshots in the existing study were visually inspected. Re-fetching the guide during this research returned HTTP 429. No Photos microinteraction timings were measured.

**Pixelmator Pro.** Official static interface screenshots and descriptions inspected; exact app animation curves not measured.

**Arc.** Fresh focus-mode video samples inspected; existing official split-view sequence inspected. Arc is a historical interaction reference, not a claim about an actively evolving product. Observed motion: Sidebar retreats and the content gains space. Split-view examples establish a spatial destination.

**Dia.** Official screenshots and authored explanations inspected; independent source supports UI praise, not measured motion.

**Raycast.** This community statement is individual opinion and predates current v2 changes; do not present it as consensus about v2.

**LookAway.** Customer opinions are anecdotal. Do not infer consensus from ratings or repeat health outcome claims. Observed motion: Fresh official samples show background softening before reminder text and supporting details.

**Craft.** Fresh seeking alone returned static checkbox frames. Actual headless playback was used to verify completion animation. No reference timing is prescribed for v_ase. Observed motion: Completion text treatment precedes the filled checkbox and visible checkmark in the sampled playback.

**CleanShot X.** Existing official demo sequence visually inspected; official feature page independently reopened. Observed motion: A capture becomes a persistent thumbnail that supports drag and follow-up actions.

**Things.** Fresh official promotional video samples inspected. Presentation-camera effects cannot establish native app timing. Observed motion: A task lifts from the list into a movable object in the promotional demo.

**Nova.** Fresh official split-video samples inspected; source praises a separate welcome animation that was not directly sampled in this study. Observed motion: A blue drop-destination preview appears before the split is committed.

**Sketch.** Supplementary source. Official UI directly inspected; not counted toward the ten independently substantiated recognition entries.

**Screen Studio.** Supplementary source. Exported-demo imagery inspected; internal native UI motion was not sufficiently observed. Marketing awards/badges are not used as independent recognition evidence.

## Review decisions

The first implementation placed documents in the navigator. User review rejected
that choice and exposed a broken browser menu, redundant native menus, heavy
canvas resizing, ambiguous mode names and category-scoped search. The revised
contract takes browser-style horizontal document tabs, a Scene-only sidebar and
a neutral default palette. Arc informs purposeful panel travel, not tab placement.

Raycast informs one global search above every workspace, keyboard access and
focus return. Pixelmator/Photos/Sketch inform compact context-sensitive forms;
View only/Edit structure visibly express exclusive access modes. Craft informs
clear selected and pressed feedback; LookAway informs deferred progress while
input isolation remains immediate. CleanShot informs the concrete exported-image
result. Nova/Things inform explicit tab-drag destinations and cancellation.

Astra xhigh reviewed actual desktop captures at 1440, 1280 and 1024 px, all eight
palette/appearance combinations, dark menus over a white scientific canvas and
both mode selections. The reviewer found and required fixes to search-popup
translation, atomic parent/child theme updates and light-theme text/focus contrast.
Measured repeated WebGL resizing during panel motion was replaced by one
committed canvas resize per toggle and independent panel travel. Headless timings
establish reduced redundant work; they do not establish native app frame rate.

Panel motion, durations and easing are chosen for v_ase and are not claimed measurements of the reference applications. Sliders, scrubbing and drag handles follow direct input. Reduced motion removes travel. Scientific coordinates, camera direction and pixels per angstrom are independent of UI presentation.
